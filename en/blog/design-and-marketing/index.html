How design affects sales

Why the same offer brings different numbers of leads in different designs.

SSofia, co-founder, LIVSKYNovember 10, 2026 · 5 min

Key points in 30 seconds

  1. Visitors decide whether to stay within seconds.
  2. Hierarchy and one main button beat trendy effects.
  3. Design the path to a lead first, visuals second.

Contents0% read

  1. 01The first seconds decide
  2. 02Hierarchy beats effects

01The first seconds decide

Visitors decide whether to stay within seconds. Design decides whether they understand the offer in that time.

Tip

The five-second test

Show your first screen to a friend for five seconds. If they can't say what you sell, simplify the offer.

02Hierarchy beats effects

Clear hierarchy, one main button and real work examples lift conversion more than trendy effects. In one project conversion went from 1.1% to 2.6% just by reordering blocks.
