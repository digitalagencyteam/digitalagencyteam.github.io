FAQ

How much does a website cost?

A landing page starts at $1000, a multi-page site with a blog at $2500, a web service at $5000. We confirm the price after the brief.

How long does it take?

A landing page takes 2 weeks, a site up to 10 pages 4–6 weeks. Stage deadlines go into the contract.

Why are extra edits paid?

Up to 5 rounds of edits per stage are included. This keeps the project on schedule and the budget predictable.

Who maintains the site after launch?

The first month of support is included. After that we can keep it on a plan or hand it over to your team.
