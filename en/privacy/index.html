Privacy policy

How we handle the data you leave on this website.

Version of November 8, 2026

What we collect

Your name or company, a contact (Telegram, WhatsApp, phone or email) and the task description you enter in the request form. The site also collects anonymous visit statistics.

Why we need it

To reply to your request and prepare an offer and estimate. Statistics help us improve the site. We never send marketing emails without your consent.

Who we share it with

Only the services we use to receive requests: a messenger and a CRM. We don't sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

How long we keep it

While we are in touch or working on your project, and no longer than three years after our last contact. We delete it earlier on request.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, correct it or request deletion. Email us at the address below and we'll reply within 10 business days.

Contact

For data questions, email hello@livsky.ru.
